About Me

The person behind the pixels.

Hi! I'm Mukrom Karunia Azza — a Game Developer based in Indonesia with 3+ years in the game industry. I've shipped 15+ titles across mobile and web, wearing multiple hats as developer, UI/UX designer, and 2D artist.

🎯 My vision is to create games that are meaningful and beneficial for as many people as possible. I believe games have the power to educate, inspire, and bring joy beyond entertainment.

Beyond building games, I'm passionate about mentoring interns and junior artists — helping them grow into confident creators who can ship their own projects.
